<h2 align="center">Products</h2>

<a href="https://s3dat.com/products/s3-browser"><img align="left" width="300" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/S3datlab/.github/main/profile/assets/card-s3-browser-v2.png" alt="S3 Browser card"></a>

### [S3 Browser](https://s3dat.com/products/s3-browser)

`macOS 14+` `Apple silicon` `Coming soon`

A native macOS browser built for the way work actually moves. Tab groups float free or dock to the window edges, workspaces pick up where you left off, and a quotation stays attached to the page it came from.

A cinematic Home brings saved sites and recorded viewing together, and Continue Watching resumes on supported players.

<a href="https://s3dat.com/products/s3-brain"><img align="right" width="300" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/S3datlab/.github/main/profile/assets/card-s3-brain-v2.png" alt="S3 Brain card"></a>

### [S3 Brain](https://s3dat.com/products/s3-brain)

`macOS` `Local-first` `Coming soon`

Shared, source-backed memory for AI coding on your Mac. A new session should remember why the last one made a decision.

S3 Brain keeps proposals, decisions and test results apart, each linked to the conversation it came from. It works with Claude Code and other AI coding tools, and the memory lives on your Mac.

<a href="https://s3dat.com/products/clidna"><img align="left" width="300" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/S3datlab/.github/main/profile/assets/card-clidna-v2.png" alt="Clidna card"></a>

### [Clidna](https://s3dat.com/products/clidna)

`iPhone` `English · Turkish` `In development`

A fictional AI character with a life of her own. She texts first, tells you about her day and remembers what you say.

Her Memory Notebook shows every memory with its source in something you actually said, and you can correct it or make her forget. Made for adults (18+).

<br clear="left">

<a href="https://s3dat.com/products/qrestige"><img align="right" width="300" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/S3datlab/.github/main/profile/assets/card-qrestige-v2.png" alt="QRESTIGE card"></a>

### [QRESTIGE](https://s3dat.com/products/qrestige)

`Web` `iOS & Android business app` `In development`

A multi-tenant QR menu and operations platform for restaurants, cafés and lounges. The guest sees a menu; the team sees a table, a request and the next thing to do.

QRESTIGE connects the floor, kitchen, bar and manager in one operation, with a visual Menu Theme Studio for the guest-facing menu.

### [LIFTJAB](https://s3dat.com/products/liftjab)

`iOS 18+` `In development`

A native iPhone training app where real discipline becomes a personal pixel gym and avatar.

Plan routines, log sets with timers and keep your full history. The work you log builds the world around you, and earned status stays separate from purchased style.
